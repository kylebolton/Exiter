A cleaner feed: quoted posts, badges, cards and spacing now match the rest of the theme.

- **Quote cards** are flat hairline cards with a small avatar and a single-line name, handle and time.
- **Verified badges** are neutral line icons instead of X blue, and bio links and mentions follow the theme too.
- **Link cards, polls and the profile hover card** share the same hairline style; "Profile Summary" is now an 8px-radius outline button.
- **Cleaner post layout:** the action row lines up with the post text and media, there is more room under images and video, and the name, handle and time sit on one centre line.
- **Density** gains a third option: Compact, Comfortable or Airy (Settings > Layout).
- **Profile and Notifications** get matching type, muted meta text and hover states.
- The new-posts pill centres the poster avatars.

### Updating
Use **Check for Updates…** in Exiter, or download a build below.

### Downloads
- **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): `Exiter-0.4.0-mac-universal.dmg`. Signed and notarized.
- **Windows** (x64): `Exiter-0.4.0-win-x64.exe`. Not code-signed; SmartScreen will warn.
- **Linux** (x64): `Exiter-0.4.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `Exiter-0.4.0-linux-amd64.deb`.

Windows and Linux installers are cross-built and have not been tested on those operating systems. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
