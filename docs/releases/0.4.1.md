A white Exiter app icon, a stable navigation rail, and a feed that makes full use of your window.

- **New app icon:** a white tile with a smaller, softer charcoal X, with matching artwork in the GitHub README.
- **Fixed navigation:** the 88px icon rail stays pinned to the left at every window width. The Post button shows exactly one icon, including when X changes its navigation layout.
- **Expanding feed:** Fill uses the available width beside the navigation, including after resizing or changing pages. Choose **Settings → Layout → Feed width → Fill**; Medium and Narrow remain available for a limited reading width.
- **Image viewer:** the close cross has the correct size, contrast, hover and keyboard-focus states. It sits at the top right of the image area, clear of the macOS window buttons and the post reply panel.
- Fixed settings arriving before the page's document element is ready.

### Updating
Use **Check for Updates…** in Exiter, or download a build below.

### Downloads
- **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): `Exiter-0.4.1-mac-universal.dmg`. Signed and notarized.
- **Windows** (x64): `Exiter-0.4.1-win-x64.exe`. Not code-signed; SmartScreen will warn.
- **Linux** (x64): `Exiter-0.4.1-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `Exiter-0.4.1-linux-amd64.deb`.

Windows and Linux installers are cross-built and have not been tested on those operating systems. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
