Fixes the sidebar breaking when a notification arrives.

- Unread notification and message badges now keep the compact icon rail at the correct width, leaving the timeline its full space.
- The Post button and account controls stay aligned when badges appear, change, or clear. The wider labelled sidebar still works normally.

### Updating
Use **Check for Updates…** in Exiter, or download a build below.

### Downloads
- **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): `Exiter-0.3.1-mac-universal.dmg`. Signed and notarized.
- **Windows** (x64): `Exiter-0.3.1-win-x64.exe`. Not code-signed; SmartScreen will warn.
- **Linux** (x64): `Exiter-0.3.1-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `Exiter-0.3.1-linux-amd64.deb`.

Windows and Linux installers are cross-built and have not been tested on those operating systems. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
